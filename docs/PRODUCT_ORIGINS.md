# Product origins

Select products by exact product, part, metric and term, then group by Order number (default), CSI or Support contract number. Software support is the default financial scope; hardware support and subscriptions remain separate. Global filters find matching groups, never remove their companion source lines. Group drilldowns show every Active cleansed record carrying the exact identifier, including other portfolios and countries, plus other rows on the same unambiguous underlying orders when using CSI or contract grouping. No one-to-one identifier hierarchy is assumed.

Selected-product share is selected annual fees divided by total fees within the chosen financial scope. Other portfolios remain visible and flagged, outside that percentage. Missing fees, unresolved FX, nonpositive denominators and negative fees produce an unknown percentage. Missing group IDs remain separate per source line. Multi-ID cells remain literal with a review flag, with no fee splitting or allocation. Unknown shares remain visible under minimum-share thresholds. Quantity values retain their source metric and are not added across unlike metrics.

CSV exports contain group identifiers, underlying orders, source references, fee composition, currency, date and structural flags. Individual source references open the existing original-workbook evidence dialog. Saved reports retain the full cleansed source and reopen with product selections and thresholds cleared.

This view traces commercial source relationships; it does not infer deployment, license-set membership, contractual independence, termination rights or achievable savings. Its scope cannot exceed the provided cleansed extract. Review license-set dependencies, applicable orders and contractual exceptions separately. Oracle Software Technical Support Policies sections License Set, Matching Service Levels and Pricing Following Reduction of Licenses or Support Level provide context: https://www.oracle.com/contracts/docs/057419.pdf . No policy calculation or assumed repricing is applied.
